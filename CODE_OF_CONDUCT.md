# Code of Conduct

## Expected conduct

Be respectful, specific, and evidence-driven. Critique code and claims rather
than people. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, expose private information,
or publish credentials, private skill contents, or exploit details.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to project repositories, discussions, reviews, and other
spaces represented as Hermes Jack-In community spaces. Maintainers may edit or
remove contributions, restrict participation, or refer platform-policy breaches
to GitHub.

For urgent safety concerns, use the private process in `SECURITY.md`. For
harassment or abuse, use GitHub's reporting controls without reposting harmful
content publicly. Enforcement decisions may be appealed privately when an
appropriate private channel is available.
